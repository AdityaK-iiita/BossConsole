# BOSS v9.4.12

**Release Date:** 2026-08-15

A sign-in release. The auth card spanned the whole window and an empty form showed a field with no button to press - both defects lived in shared code, so all four auth screens carried them - and the space the card was wrongly filling is now a brand panel.

> **macOS 13.0 (Ventura) or later is required**, unchanged from v9.4.0 - the bundled JxBrowser 9.4.0 engine declares `LSMinimumSystemVersion 13.0`.

## Highlights

- **The sign-in card stretched across the entire window.** `AuthCard` wrote `Modifier.fillMaxWidth().widthIn(max = 400.dp)`, and the 400.dp cap was silently discarded: `fillMaxWidth` measures its child with `minWidth == maxWidth == the parent's width`, and `widthIn` enforces incoming constraints, so it coerced its own 0..400 range back up into that fixed range and returned the parent width. Same rule the repo adopted in `5bb14ea0` and encoded in `DialogCardWidthConventionTest` - that guard misses this one because `AuthCard` is not a `BossDialog` call site (#181)
- **Continue is now always rendered while the email step is open**, disabled until the address is worth submitting. Hiding it until the text contained an `@` left the card with nothing to act on and a 64.dp gap where the button belonged, and made the form change height as you typed (#181)
- **`AuthScaffold` replaces the frame all four screens repeated verbatim**, which is how three of them had a `verticalScroll` and the fourth - the one whose content grows with the number of registered passkeys - did not. Wide windows get a brand panel beside a fixed 520.dp form pane; below 900.dp the form goes full width and carries the wordmark itself (#181)
- **The brand panel is decoration that cannot take the sign-in screen down with it.** Every piece is independently guarded: a disabled flag, a missing Chromium engine, a failed render and an absent script all land on the drawn art underneath, which is a complete panel on its own (#181)

## Features

- **auth**: a brand panel drawn from bossconsole.ai's visual language through the design system, since Blueprint's KDoc records that its tokens are lifted from the site's stylesheet - the 24/72px grid, the radial signal glow, the dot lattice and the vignette, with alphas expressed against tokens so the grid is a dark hairline on the light themes rather than an invisible one. Copy is the site's own (#181)
- **auth**: on top of the art, a bundled offline copy of the site's hero renders by default - local rather than the live URL, because loading bossconsole.ai put a network fetch on the one screen a user cannot get past, so a blank pane was likeliest exactly when the network was bad, and it was a fully interactive page with no chrome where one click navigated away with no way back. `BOSS_AUTH_BRAND_SITE=false` (also `0`, `no`, `off`) or `-Dboss.auth.brand.site=false` turns it off, matching the vocabulary of the browser-telemetry kill switch; the off switch is kept because rendering the page starts a Chromium engine (#181)
- **auth**: the panel responds - a Matrix-style digit rain, a drawn cursor, planets carrying the capability chips around their orbits, and a preview dialog behind each one, built from the site's own console chrome with the site's own copy. The orbits hold still while the pointer is over a chip or planet, while one has keyboard focus, and while a preview is open, because aiming at a drifting target is the one genuinely irritating thing about putting controls in orbit (#181)
- **auth**: the screen opens with a zoom reveal and an arrival swell - the panel arrives zoomed and centred, settles back to its resting place, then the copy and art follow; the rain holds off until that is finished, gated on the reveal's own `animationend` rather than a matching timeout, so the duration lives in one place. The four-second low swell is synthesised in the JVM, so there is no asset to ship and no licensed material compiled into the app, and `BOSS_AUTH_SOUND=false` silences it (#181)
- **auth**: the caret starts in the email field, so signing in never needs the mouse. Opt-in per call site, so the magic-link screen's manual field does not steal focus, and `requestFocus` is guarded - it throws if the node is not attached yet, and a sign-in screen crashing on the way to focusing a field would be a poor trade for saving one click (#181)

## Bug Fixes

- **auth**: the card honours its 400.dp cap, and the column is centred. `BoxWithConstraints` defaults to `TopStart` and the `Column` had no `fillMaxWidth`, which the full-width card hid - capping the width alone would have shipped a 400.dp card against the left edge, worse than the bug (#181)
- **auth**: Chromium was booted on the UI thread. `FluckEngine.engine.newBrowser()` ran directly in a `LaunchedEffect` body, which is the composition dispatcher - the EDT - and that getter is a synchronous multi-second boot under a lock, so it froze the sign-in screen, email field included, for the duration. The startup prewarm does not cover it: prewarm returns early when no browser profile directory exists, which is exactly the first-ever launch that shows this screen, and mid-prewarm it would block on the same lock anyway. Now on `Dispatchers.IO` with only the state write on the composition thread (#181)
- **auth**: the catch missed the failure it was written for. `catch (e: Exception)` lets `UnsatisfiedLinkError` straight through - a `LinkageError`, and the characteristic failure of a Chromium bundle that does not match this build - so an engine failure escaped composition instead of ending at the drawn art. It catches `Throwable` now, and rethrows `CancellationException` rather than reporting normal disposal as a failure (#181)
- **auth**: `BrowserViewState` construction is guarded and reports through `onFailed` like every other failure. It sits outside the catch around engine creation, and "Can't obtain the display ID of a closed window" thrown while attaching the view escaped composition - on the sign-in screen that is a crashed sign-in screen, the one outcome this decoration must never cause (#181)
- **auth**: the browser could leak. It was created in a `LaunchedEffect` but closed by a `DisposableEffect` keyed on a value that is null until the recomposition *after* creation, so a panel leaving composition in that window - a resize under 900dp, a navigation - left a Chromium instance open with nothing holding a reference. One effect owns it end to end now: created there, closed in its own `finally` after `awaitCancellation` (#181)
- **auth**: nested same-axis scrolling on the passkey screen. Its list was a `LazyColumn` capped at 400dp inside the scaffold's `verticalScroll`, so a wheel gesture over the list scrolled the inner one and never chained out - on the screen the scaffold's KDoc singles out as needing the outer scroll most. A plain `Column` with `forEach`; laziness bought nothing for a handful of passkeys (#181)
- **auth**: the grid's emphasis lines could silently vanish. `drawBlueprintGrid` walked `x += minor` and tested `x % major < 1f`, and the accumulated float drifts, so a major line could land just below a multiple and render as a hairline. Decided by integer index now, which is exact at any density and drops two modulos per line; the dot lattice walks the same way, so dots stay on intersections across a wide pane (#181)
- **auth**: the digit-rain canvas had never once painted. It sat at `z-index: -3` and `site.css` sets `body { background: var(--ink) }` - a negative-z child paints behind its parent's background, so the field ran every frame completely covered. The earlier particle version was invisible for its whole life and was reported as working (#181)
- **auth**: digits never disappeared. The trails used the classic technique of washing the canvas each frame with a translucent backdrop, and canvas compositing is 8-bit, so at the very low wash alpha that dimming the field required (0.013), subtracting from an already-faint pixel rounds to zero and those pixels never reach transparent - a permanent ghost layer that only accumulated, made more certain by every step towards "lighter". Each glyph carries its own age against a real `clearRect` now, so dimness and lifetime are independent knobs (#181)
- **auth**: the entrance sound had been silent since the day it was written, and was reported as working. The WebAudio version listened for `pointermove`, which is not a user-activation gesture in Chromium: the first mouse movement built a suspended `AudioContext`, played nothing, and latched its "already played" flag, so the later real click never retried. Playing from the JVM has no autoplay gate at all, and it no longer depends on the browser, so it plays when the brand page is disabled or its engine failed (#181)
- **auth**: the orbit chips had stopped being clickable. `.brand-orbit` carries `pointer-events: none` from when the wrappers were purely decorative, and the chips inherited it when they moved inside to ride the planets (#181)
- **auth**: a focusable control sat inside an `aria-hidden` subtree once the chips became buttons - both a violation and a keyboard trap, reachable by tab and invisible to assistive tech. `.hero-art` loses the attribute; its decorative children carry it individually (#181)
- **auth**: the drawn cursor used to freeze against the last edge. The panel is a native browser surface in a Compose window, so crossing to the Compose side often produces no leave event at all - it now takes `pointerleave`, `mouseleave`, a null `relatedTarget` on `mouseout`, window blur, and an edge watchdog conditioned on proximity to the viewport edge so a mouse resting mid-panel keeps its cursor (#181)

## Improvements

- **auth**: a token pass, each item fixing something specific rather than a preference. The card looked muddy because `elevation = 2.dp` makes Material 2 apply its dark-theme `LocalElevationOverlay`, so it was never the `raised` token it asked for. The surface hierarchy was inverted - page `panel`, card `raised`, field `panel` again - so the field was darker than its container in the dark themes and invisible in Blueprint Light and Daylight, where panel and raised are both pure white; fields are `ink` insets now. `PrimaryActionButton` hardcoded `Color.White` on a `signal` fill, and `onSignal` is near-black in Daylight and Operator, while the passkey button on the same screen already used the token. `AuthOptions.Invalid` rendered in `textPrimary` while `ErrorMessage` used `alert` (#181)
- **auth**: `boss_icon.png` is gone from these screens. It is a launcher tile - a near-black rounded square with BOSS set inside it - so on `ink` it has no edge and reads as a hole, and it repeated in words whatever label sat beside it; the lockup is typographic, as the site's own hero is. That left `BossLogo` unused, so it is deleted, and the asset still serves `LoadingScreen` and `OfflineScreen` (#181)
- **auth**: `AuthCard` and `AuthCardTitle` are deleted - the layout has no card surface, and a component called `AuthCard` that draws no card is worse than none. The form pane's width is fixed at 520.dp rather than a weight, because a percentage split puts the 400.dp column back in the middle of a void on a wide display (#181)
- **auth**: the "is this address worth submitting" predicate was written out inline in four places and is now one function - deliberately the *same* predicate, since tightening validation under a layout fix is how a behaviour change goes unnoticed (#181)
- **auth**: pointer parallax was built and then removed. It is motion nobody asked for every time they reach for the email field, so the scene stays where it is put and only the cursor follows the pointer. Recorded in both places so it is not reintroduced as an oversight (#181)
- **auth**: everything infinite is gated on `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, including the parallax and the sound - not softened versions of them. The sound stops with the screen through a `DisposableEffect` with a short fade, since four seconds is easily longer than a saved session takes to sign in and a swell carrying on over the signed-in app is the one intolerable outcome; a clip that finishes rendering after the screen has gone is discarded rather than started, and a `LineListener` closes the clip when it ends naturally so the audio device is not held for the life of the app (#181)
- **tests**: `AuthScaffoldLayoutTest` pins the width at 400.dp, the right-hand placement, the narrow collapse, that a window under 400.dp shrinks instead of overflowing, and the breakpoint as a pure function. It measures rather than inspects, because at 400.dp wide the broken and fixed layouts are identical - the bug only exists on a wide window. It disables the panel through a new `authBrandSiteOverride` rather than the system property, since the environment variable takes precedence over the property and a developer may have it set (#181)
- **tests**: `AuthBrandAssetsTest` pins what the vendored bundle claims about itself - that the page and every linked asset resolve, that the page references each shipped file name, that nothing reaches the network, and that there is no anchor or hero-actions block. Each of those fails silently today, and one is not hypothetical: the Google Fonts `@import` strip used `@import"[^;]*;` against a URL containing semicolons, so it cut inside the string and left broken CSS (#181)
- **auth**: dead imports left by the scaffold migration are gone - `rememberScrollState` and `verticalScroll` in all three converted screens, `RoundedCornerShape` in two, and `LazyColumn`/`items` after the passkey-list change (#181)

## Known Limitations

- **The brand panel is rebuilt per auth screen transition.** `AuthScaffold` is invoked separately in each branch of `AuthScreenContainer`'s `when`; hoisting the brand pane above it or using `movableContentOf` restructures four screens and the container, and wants its own change (#181)
- **`BrowserViewState` is still not closed on dispose**, matching `DesktopPasskeyBrowserView` - worth doing with that one rather than diverging here (#181)
